Install on iPhone or iPad

Safari has no install button — Apple requires you to add it by hand.

  1. Tap Share (the square with an arrow) in the Safari toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. SaResQ opens full-screen, without browser chrome.
