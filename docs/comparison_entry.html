Indicative Indian street prices for the thermal configuration. Two rows go to them. On endurance, SaResQ flies on a light airframe — but at roughly a tenth of the cost a district fields ten aircraft instead of one, so total time over a search area goes up, not down. On resolution, detection is driven by contrast against the scene background rather than pixel count, so fewer pixels costs range, not the ability to detect. SaResQ is a payload: it can equally fly on any of these aircraft.
