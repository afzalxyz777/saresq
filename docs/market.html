At ₹12–15 lakh a state buys one aircraft and rations it across an entire disaster. At ₹30,000 a district fields one per block. The binding constraint stops being budget and becomes training.

WHY NOW

2021The airspace opened

Drone Rules 2021 cut approvals sharply and published the airspace map, making routine district-level operation legally practical for the first time.

PLIManufacturing moved onshore

Production-linked incentives are pulling drone supply chains into India. Our bill of materials is already sourced domestically, so the policy tailwind applies directly rather than in principle.

EDGEThe hardware curve crossed

Quantised detectors now run usefully on ₹4,000 of compute. This system was not buildable at this price five years ago — what changed is the hardware, not the idea.

GAPNobody serves this tier

Enterprise vendors compete on imaging fidelity for buyers with lakhs to spend. No one is building automated survivor detection for a district budget.
